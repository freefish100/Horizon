## [First Atmosphere Detected on Rocky Exoplanet in Habitable Zone](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Astronomers using the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) have detected an atmosphere on LHS 1140b, a rocky exoplanet located 48 light-years away in its star's habitable zone. This marks the first confirmed atmosphere on an Earth-like planet in a habitable zone. This discovery is a major milestone in exoplanet research, as it opens the door to studying the atmospheres of potentially habitable rocky worlds. It provides a target for future missions searching for biosignatures and understanding planetary habitability beyond our solar system. The atmosphere was detected using transmission spectroscopy as LHS 1140b transited its host star, ruling out a mini-Neptune interpretation. Located in the habitable zone of a red dwarf star, the planet is about 1.7 times Earth's radius and may be a water world or have a nitrogen-rich atmosphere.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: Exoplanet atmospheres are studied by analyzing starlight that passes through the planet's atmosphere during a transit, revealing the chemical fingerprints of molecules. The James Webb Space Telescope, launched in 2021, is optimized for infrared observations and can characterize the atmospheres of potentially habitable exoplanets. The habitable zone is the region around a star where temperatures could allow liquid water on a planet's surface.

## [Huawei Unveils Ascend 950 Supernode, Claims 6.7x NVIDIA Compute](https://www.ithome.com/0/978/019.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

At WAIC 2026, Huawei publicly demonstrated the Ascend 950 supernode (Atlas 950 SuperPoD) for the first time, based on the Lingqu interconnect protocol and supernode architecture. It supports a maximum of 1024 cards and delivers 1 EFLOPS FP8 and 2 EFLOPS FP4 compute power, with 256 TB of unified global memory, claiming 6.7 times the total compute power of NVIDIA's NVL144 system with 144 GPUs. This announcement poses a significant challenge to NVIDIA's dominance in AI hardware, particularly in the Chinese market where domestic alternatives are critical. If validated, the Ascend 950 could redefine large-scale AI training and inference capabilities, potentially accelerating AI adoption in China and reducing reliance on foreign chips. The Ascend 950 employs the Lingqu interconnect protocol with six-layer reliability mechanisms and sub-microsecond latency for high-speed inter-node communication. Additionally, the Ascend 384 supernode has already been commercially deployed in over 750 systems across internet, telecom, and finance sectors, and is the only supernode in China that has trained SOTA models.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 17, 10:27

**Background**: Supernode architecture is a next-generation computing design that uses high-speed interconnect protocols (like NVLink, CXL, or Huawei's Lingqu) to directly connect accelerators (GPUs/NPUs) beyond traditional CPU-centric nodes, forming a high-bandwidth, low-latency cluster that behaves as a single logical machine. Huawei's Lingqu protocol, introduced in 2025, enables reliable 200-meter transmission and 2.1 microsecond latency for large-scale AI clusters. This contrasts with NVIDIA's approach using NVLink and InfiniBand, and the claimed 6.7x advantage is based on a comparison with NVIDIA's NVL144 system with 144 GPUs.

## [Stereo2Spatial: Diffusion Model for Binaural Audio Conversion](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1uzevbg/stereo2spatial_convert_stereo_music_tracks_to/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The user released a diffusion model called Stereo2Spatial that converts stereo music tracks into spatialized binaural mixes, using a custom VAE (EAR-VAE) and memory tokens for long-context stability, and later pivoted to a waveform-based model with amplitude lifting to resolve training instability. This project addresses the practical need for high-quality spatial audio conversion, making spatial music more accessible. The memory token technique and amplitude lifting method contribute novel solutions to common challenges in audio diffusion models. The model was trained on 7,669 tracks for ~20 days using 2x A6000 GPUs, with stage one training on 10-34 second sequences and stage two on 122-second sequences. The waveform version supports optional mix-style conditioning and outputs direct binaural audio.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/kittenkrazy · Jul 17, 22:55

**Background**: Spatial audio creates an immersive listening experience by placing sounds in a 3D space, often using binaural rendering for headphones. Diffusion models generate data by gradually denoising random noise, while VAEs compress audio into a latent space. Memory tokens help the model maintain context across long audio windows, and amplitude lifting stabilizes raw waveform training by normalizing RMS levels.

She also introduced a four-dimension framework to evaluate cost-effectiveness beyond token pricing. This metric provides a more meaningful way for enterprises to assess AI investments, focusing on the value of work completed per dollar spent rather than raw token costs. It could drive more efficient model selection and deployment strategies across the industry. The framework includes four dimensions: useful work completed, full cost per successful task, AI output reliability, and whether per-dollar input generates more value as usage grows.
