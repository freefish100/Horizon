## [Generative design of viable bacteriophage genomes with Evo 1 and Evo 2](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate whole bacteriophage genomes based on the lytic phage ΦX174, and experimentally validated that 16 of the AI-designed phages were viable. This marks the first generative design of viable bacteriophage genomes. This work demonstrates that genome-scale language models can produce functional biological sequences, opening new avenues for AI-driven synthetic biology and custom phage design with potential applications in antimicrobial therapy and biotechnology. It also advances the frontier of generative AI in whole-genome engineering. The design template was the well-studied lytic phage ΦX174, and the generated genomes exhibited realistic genetic architecture and desired host tropism. The 16 viable phages showed substantial evolutionary novelty, meaning they were not merely near-copies of the template sequence.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models apply natural language processing techniques to genomic sequences, treating DNA as a language that can be learned and generated. Evo 1 and Evo 2 are frontier genome language models trained on large-scale genomic datasets. Bacteriophages are viruses that infect bacteria, and ΦX174 is a small, well-characterized lytic phage often used as a model organism. This research tests whether generative language models can design complete functional genomes rather than just individual genetic parts.

## [GitHub Models Retired, Breaking CI/CD LLM Workflows](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/9/github-models-is-now-retired/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

GitHub Models was officially retired on July 30, 2026, removing its playground, model catalog, inference API, and BYOK feature for all customers. Simon Willison's GitHub Actions workflow failed due to the retirement, and he replaced it with an OpenAI API key using GPT-5.6 Luna. This is significant because GitHub Models provided a unified API for multiple LLM providers and a free way for GitHub Actions to run prompts using the existing GitHub API key. Its retirement disrupts Continuous AI workflows and forces developers to migrate to other providers, potentially increasing costs and complexity. The retirement was announced via a GitHub changelog on July 30, 2026, and the brownout message even became stale after the retirement completed. Willison speculates that coding-agent patterns made free or subsidized tokens prohibitively expensive, though GitHub did not state the reason officially.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 9, 22:48

**Background**: GitHub Models was a platform for experimenting with AI models via a web playground and a unified inference API, providing access to models from OpenAI, DeepSeek, Meta, Microsoft, xAI, and others. Its key advantage was that GitHub Actions could use the repository's existing GitHub API key to call LLMs, enabling Continuous AI patterns where background agents handle reasoning tasks in CI workflows, similar to how CI/CD automates rule-based tasks.

## [World's Largest Single AI Computing Hub Starts Operating in Inner Mongolia](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367666.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On August 6, Envision Group announced the official commissioning of the 'Envision Ulanqab Galaxy Base' in Inner Mongolia. The facility covers 120,000 square meters, supports one million GPU parallel computing, has a planned total capacity of 2GW, and uses over 80% green electricity, making it the world's largest single AI computing facility. This milestone marks a major scale-up in AI infrastructure, especially for China's domestic computing ecosystem. The facility's low-latency connection to Beijing (4.2ms) and cheaper electricity make it a strategic hub for large-scale AI model training and token production. Ulanqab is one of the eight national 'East Data West Computing' nodes, and the base is the first flagship project of Envision's 'Gobi Mission' plan, which aims to provide replicable solutions for domestic computing clusters. Companies such as Huawei, Alibaba, Apple, and Kuaishou have already deployed computing facilities in this region.

**Background**: The 'East Data West Computing' (东数西算) project is a Chinese national initiative to develop data centers in western regions, where renewable energy and land are abundant, to process data generated by economic activities in eastern China. This facility's emphasis on token-output capacity reflects a growing industry shift toward measuring AI data center efficiency in 'tokens per watt' rather than traditional power metrics. Green electricity (over 80% here) and proximity to Beijing make Ulanqab an attractive site for next-generation AI infrastructure.

## [Musk Unveils Plan for Robotic Lunar Factory to Build AI Satellites](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/pure-insanity-elon-musk-details-173635969.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

During SpaceX's first public earnings call, Elon Musk announced a plan to build an automated factory on the Moon, using Starship to deliver equipment and robots that extract minerals from lunar soil to mass-produce AI computing satellites. The finished satellites would be launched directly from the lunar surface via an electromagnetic 'mass driver.' This visionary plan could dramatically lower satellite launch costs by using lunar materials and a mass driver instead of rocket launches from Earth. If realized, it would position SpaceX at the center of lunar industrialization and in-space manufacturing, reshaping the space economy and future AI infrastructure. The plan relies on in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) to extract aluminum, titanium, and silicon from lunar regolith, which is technically challenging because lunar dust is abrasive and the Moon experiences 14-day cycles of light and darkness. Former SpaceX VP Jim Cantrell called the plan 'pure insanity' but believes Musk can accomplish it, while industry experts generally see technical feasibility yet caution that Musk's timelines tend to be overly optimistic.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 05:37

**Background**: A mass driver is a proposed non-rocket space launch system that uses a linear motor or electromagnetic coils to accelerate payloads to high speeds; on the Moon, a stationary mass driver could take advantage of the lack of atmosphere and lower gravity to launch materials into orbit. In-situ resource utilization (ISRU) refers to using materials found on celestial bodies, such as lunar soil for metals, oxygen, or water, to reduce the need to transport supplies from Earth. Starship is SpaceX's fully reusable super-heavy-lift launch vehicle, which Musk envisions as the primary transport for lunar cargo and construction equipment.

## [Apple Lobbies White House to Buy Chips From Blacklisted Chinese Memory Maker](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43083) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple is lobbying the Trump administration for approval or assurance to purchase memory chips from ChangXin Memory (CXMT), a Chinese manufacturer on the U.S. military's blacklist. Apple is not currently barred by law, but wants protection in case CXMT is added to the Entity List, mainly to ease rising memory costs that have already forced MacBook and iPad price increases. This could mark a significant shift in U.S.-China tech supply chains, as Apple is a high-profile buyer and its endorsement of a blacklisted Chinese chipmaker may face strong political backlash. The outcome will test how the Trump administration balances trade talks, rare-earth negotiations, and national security hawks in shaping semiconductor policy. The White House has delayed some new tech restrictions amid trade and rare-earth talks, but Congress and security hawks likely oppose increasing Apple's dependence on Chinese memory supply. Apple has already raised MacBook and iPad prices because of "unsustainable" memory costs, and it remains unclear whether the government will give explicit backing.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 10, 01:15

**Background**: ChangXin Memory (CXMT) is a Chinese DRAM maker on the Pentagon's "Chinese military companies" blacklist, a designation that mainly restricts U.S. investment but does not itself ban commercial purchases. The Commerce Department's Entity List is a separate, stricter export-control tool: once a company is placed on it, suppliers need a license that is typically denied, effectively cutting off U.S.-origin goods and technology. Memory chips (DRAM) are essential components in Apple products, and DRAM prices have risen sharply, which is why Apple is seeking assurance rather than waiting for a future listing.
