## [GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra Produces Proof of Cycle Double Cover Conjecture](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/04d1d1e4-bc75-476a-97cf-49055cd98d31/cdc_proof.pdf) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On July 10, 2026, OpenAI released a preprint claiming that their GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra model generated a proof of the Cycle Double Cover Conjecture, a major open problem in graph theory that has stood for decades. This marks a significant milestone in AI's ability to tackle long-standing mathematical conjectures, potentially accelerating discovery and changing how research is conducted. If verified, it would demonstrate that large language models can produce novel, non-trivial proofs beyond simple pattern matching. The proof is reportedly very concise, suggesting it may exploit a clever trick missed by human experts. However, its correctness has not yet been verified by the mathematical community, and the prompt used to guide the model was also released for analysis.

hackernews · scrlk · Jul 10, 18:29 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48863490)

**Background**: The Cycle Double Cover Conjecture, posed by Tutte, Itai, Rodeh, Szekeres, and Seymour, asserts that every bridgeless undirected graph has a collection of cycles that each edge lies in exactly twice. It is a fundamental problem in graph theory related to graph embeddings and has been open for decades. Previous attempts by humans had not yielded a complete proof.

## [QuadRF: Open-Source RF Camera Sees WiFi Through Walls](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/quadrf-can-spot-drones-and-see-wifi-through-my-wall/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

QuadRF is an open-source 4x4 MIMO software-defined radio platform that visualizes radio frequency signals in real time, capable of detecting drones and mapping WiFi access points through walls. This tool democratizes RF sensing technology previously limited to government agencies, enabling hobbyists and security researchers to perform spatial RF analysis for drone detection, wireless security auditing, and signal mapping. QuadRF uses a Raspberry Pi 5 with four coherent transmit/receive channels and swappable dual-polarization antennas, providing a browser-based interface for color-coded RF visualization.

**Background**: Software-defined radio (SDR) allows flexible processing of radio signals using software instead of dedicated hardware. Phased-array systems use multiple antennas to determine the direction of incoming signals. QuadRF combines these technologies into an accessible kit for education and experimentation.

## [Nilay Patel: AR Glasses Require Always-On Cameras, Cloud Processing](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/10/nilay-patel/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

In a recent Vergecast episode, Nilay Patel argued that augmented reality glasses inherently require always-on cameras and cloud-based processing, making privacy invasion unavoidable and potentially not worth the societal trade-off. This critique challenges the core premise of consumer AR glasses, suggesting that the privacy costs may outweigh the benefits. It forces the industry to confront fundamental design trade-offs between functionality and user trust. Patel notes that no chip small enough to fit in a glasses stem can simultaneously be powerful and energy-efficient enough for real-time processing, necessitating cloud offloading. He compares this to the Apple Vision Pro, which uses an external battery pack and is much larger.

**Background**: Augmented reality glasses overlay digital information onto the real world, requiring continuous camera input to understand the environment through techniques like Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM). On-device AI processing is power-hungry, so many AR devices offload computation to the cloud, raising privacy concerns about continuous video transmission.

## [OpenAI, Google Reportedly Serve Blacklisted Chinese Firms via Singapore](https://www.ft.com/content/5d6aafa1-5d47-4585-aa95-6ec06a6cd20f) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Financial Times reports that OpenAI and Google have been providing advanced AI models to the Singaporean subsidiaries of Alibaba, Baidu, and Tencent, whose parent companies are on the US Department of Defense's 1260H list of Chinese military companies. This exposes a potential loophole in US export controls, as current restrictions do not broadly prohibit Chinese companies from accessing AI models outside mainland China, reigniting calls for tighter AI export regulations. OpenAI suspended API access for Alibaba-affiliated users last month after detecting suspected 'model distillation' and reported the activity to the US government; Anthropic, by contrast, has a stricter policy that fully blocks Chinese companies and their overseas entities from accessing its frontier AI models.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 10, 09:59

**Background**: The 1260H list, updated annually under Section 1260H of the FY2021 NDAA, designates entities as 'Chinese military companies' operating directly or indirectly in the US. Model distillation (knowledge distillation) is a technique where knowledge from a large AI model is transferred to a smaller one, potentially enabling efficient replication of frontier models.
