The reported hallucination rate for Astra first dropped from 4.2% to 2%, then was restored to 4.2%, while the ExploitBench score for GPT-5.6 Sol was raised from 5.5% to 11.5% and Anthropic's Fable 5.1 math score was temporarily cut by about 10 percentage points.
